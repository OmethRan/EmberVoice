Ember Voice

Tap the orb and talk. Ember answers out loud and shows the reply on screen.

Try asking for the weather, a timer, a quick calculation, a conversion, or who someone was. Say “remember that…” to keep a note.

On a phone, shake it and Ember wobbles, looks confused, then shakes its head to fix its face.

Paste an ElevenLabs key and voice ID to use a much richer voice. Leave blank to use your phone's built-in voice.

Smarter answers

Ember handles lookups, weather, math, timers and notes by herself. For open conversation she uses an AI model.

The free AI is a public online service. Everything you say, any file you attach, your name, and your saved notes and memories are sent to it on every turn, so keep private things out. It can be slow and may change or disappear. Your own key is more reliable.

Your key is saved only in this browser on this device. Anyone who can open this page on your device can use it.
